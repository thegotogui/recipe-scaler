Weights are typical values for the ingredient as commonly measured (spooned & leveled for dry goods) — brand, humidity, and packing all shift the real number by a few percent. When it matters, weigh it.

Diamond Crystal and Morton kosher salt are listed separately because their crystal shapes give the same cup a very different weight — using the wrong one in a bread or brine recipe changes the salt level substantially.
